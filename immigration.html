Immigration Services

Every immigration journey starts with a form. We handle what comes after.

Each service below is labeled with the government form or forum it typically begins with, because knowing which process applies to you is the first step in understanding your own case.

01 · Family-Based Immigration

Keeping families together

U.S. citizens and permanent residents can petition for spouses, children, parents, and siblings. The right path depends on the relationship, the family member's location, and their immigration history.

Form I-130

Family-Based Petitions

We prepare and document petitions for spouses, fiancé(e)s, children, parents, and siblings, and we track priority dates in the Visa Bulletin so you know where your family stands.

Form I-485 · I-751

Marriage-Based Green Cards & Adjustment of Status

Becoming a permanent resident from inside the U.S. means proving a genuine marriage, eligibility, and admissibility. We build complete, well-documented filings, prepare you for the interview, and handle removal of conditions.

DS-260 · NVC

Consular Processing

When a family member is abroad, the case moves through the National Visa Center and a U.S. embassy or consulate. We manage civil documents, the affidavit of support, and interview preparation.

02 · Employment & Business Immigration

Business immigration & investor visas

Entrepreneurs, investors, professionals, and the employers who sponsor them rely on a range of temporary and permanent categories. We advise both companies and individuals on eligibility, timing, and long-term strategy.

Investor Visa · E-2

E-2 Treaty Investor Visas

For nationals of treaty countries who invest a substantial amount of capital in a real, operating U.S. business they develop and direct. We help structure the investment, prepare the business plan, and build the case from source of funds to consular interview.

Full E-2 visa guide →
Form I-140 · PERM

EB-2 / EB-3 Employment-Based Green Cards

Permanent residence for professionals with advanced degrees or exceptional ability (EB-2), including the National Interest Waiver, and for skilled workers and professionals (EB-3). We guide employers and employees through labor certification, the I-140, and adjustment or consular processing.

Form I-129

H-1B & Other Employment Visas

Specialty-occupation H-1B petitions, transfers, and extensions, along with other employment-based nonimmigrant options. H-1B rules, fees, and selection procedures have changed significantly in recent years, so we review current requirements with every employer and worker before filing.

Form I-129 · O-1

O-1 Extraordinary Ability Visas

For individuals with extraordinary ability in the sciences, arts, education, business, or athletics. These cases rise or fall on evidence. We help you identify, organize, and present your achievements in the way the regulations require.

03 · Asylum & Humanitarian Immigration

Protection, relief & waivers

Humanitarian options exist to protect people fleeing persecution, violence, or abuse. Waivers can overcome past problems that would otherwise block a green card. These are sensitive cases, and we handle them with discretion and urgency.

Form I-589

Asylum

Affirmative asylum before USCIS and defensive asylum in immigration court, along with withholding of removal and Convention Against Torture protection. Strict filing deadlines apply, so early advice matters.

TPS · VAWA · U Visa

Humanitarian Relief

Temporary Protected Status, VAWA self-petitions for survivors of abuse by a U.S. citizen or permanent resident family member, and U visas for victims of qualifying crimes who help law enforcement.

I-601 · I-601A · I-212

Waivers

Unlawful presence, certain criminal history, misrepresentation, and prior removals can make someone inadmissible. Where a waiver is available, we build the hardship and discretionary case needed to overcome the bar.

04 · Citizenship & Immigration Court

Becoming a citizen & defending your future

Naturalization is the final step for many clients. Removal proceedings are the most serious. We handle both with careful preparation.

Form N-400 · N-600

Citizenship & Naturalization

From the eligibility review to the civics interview and oath ceremony, we prepare clients for every stage of naturalization, including cases complicated by travel history, taxes, arrests, or old records. We also handle certificates of citizenship for those who derived citizenship through a parent.

EOIR · Immigration Court

Immigration Court & Removal Defense

A Notice to Appear is not the end of the road. We represent clients in immigration court and pursue every available defense, including cancellation of removal, adjustment of status, asylum, bond, motions to reopen, and appeals to the Board of Immigration Appeals.
