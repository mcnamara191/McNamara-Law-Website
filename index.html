A visa is permission to enter the U.S. for a specific purpose and time, such as work, study, or a visit. A green card (lawful permanent residence) lets you live and work in the U.S. indefinitely, but it can be lost. Citizenship is permanent and brings the right to vote and a U.S. passport.
